About AlpenGlanz

Local. Reliable. Flexible.

We're a Schladming-based team, on the job across the region every day – for landlords, chalets and holiday apartments that want to rely on cleanliness.

Locally rooted

As a Schladming-based business, we know the local conditions, the routes and what guests expect – short distances, fast response times.

Reliable

Fixed appointments, proven checklists and clear communication – you always know your rooms will be ready on time.

Flexible

Last-minute changeovers, seasonal peaks or ongoing care – we adapt to your calendar, not the other way around.

Out and about every day

On the job in Schladming every day

Whether it's a weekend, a public holiday or the middle of peak season: our team is out across Schladming and the surrounding area every day, keeping holiday apartments, chalets and buildings clean. So landlords can count on every handover being punctual and flawless.

As your direct points of contact – no call centre, no runaround – we're personally at your side.
