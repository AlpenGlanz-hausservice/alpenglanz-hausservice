Service 01

Holiday Apartment Cleaning

A smooth turnaround between two bookings is our core business. We clean your holiday apartment right on schedule after check-out, so it's flawless and ready in time for the next check-in.

That saves you the stress before arrivals – and your guests start out in a spotless place. On request, we'll also make the beds and restock consumables.

Send enquiry

What's included

  • Kitchen including appliances, sink and worktops
  • Bathroom and WC hygienically cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, dust removed
  • Bed-making and laundry by arrangement
  • Rubbish removed, consumables restocked (on request)
  • Visual and photo check against a checklist

Ideal for owners of holiday apartments, chalets and apartments with frequent guest changeovers.

FAQ

Good to know

Answers to the questions landlords and owners ask us most often.

How quickly do you clean between check-out and check-in?
We schedule the turnaround precisely and clean within the window between your guests' departure and arrival. Tell us your handover times – we'll work around them.
Which areas do you cover?
In Schladming and the surrounding area – including Ramsau am Dachstein, Rohrmoos, Haus im Ennstal, Gröbming and Pichl. Just ask about your location.
Are short-notice or recurring appointments possible?
Yes. We take on one-off jobs as well as ongoing care throughout the season, adapting to your calendar.
How do you ensure consistent quality?
We work to fixed checklists and, on request, document the handover with photos – for reliable results on every visit.
How do I get a quote?
Just describe your property, its size and the service you need – via the contact form, by email or WhatsApp. We'll get back to you promptly with a tailored quote.
Do I have a dedicated point of contact?
Yes. As a Schladming-based business, the owners look after you personally – no call centre, no runaround.

Can't find the right service?

Get in touch – we'll put together a bespoke cleaning package for your holiday apartment, chalet or building.
